Rinjani Trekking Summit: The Ultimate Complete Expedition (4 Days 3 Nights)

4 Days 3 Nights

Embark on the ultimate Mount Rinjani experience. If you want to witness the sheer majesty of Lombok's most famous volcano without feeling rushed, our 4 Days 3 Nights trekking package is the absolute perfect choice. While shorter treks require you to push your physical limits day after day, this 4-day expedition is designed for a more comfortable, relaxed pace, giving your body more time to acclimatize and recover.

This comprehensive traverse begins in the wide savannas of Sembalun, taking you to the absolute summit (3,726m) for a glorious sunrise. You will then descend into the massive caldera to spend a full afternoon camping next to the stunning Segara Anak Crater Lake, enjoying rejuvenating soaks in the natural hot springs. The journey concludes with a trek through the dense, tropical rainforests of Senaru. This is the complete expedition for those who want to savor every spectacular landscape Rinjani has to offer.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1 - Sembalun Village to Crater Rim

  • 06:00 – 07:00: Breakfast at your hotel/homestay in Senaru or Sembalun.
  • 07:30 – 08:30: Transfer to Sembalun Village (if staying in Senaru) and register at the Rinjani Trekking Centre (RTC) at 1,051m.
  • 08:30: Begin the trek through the open, grassy savanna of the Sembalun valley.
  • 12:00: Stop at Pos 2 (Tengengean, 1,500m) for a well-deserved lunch prepared by your porters.
  • 17:00: Tackle the steep "Regret Hills" to reach Sembalun Crater Rim (2,639m). Enjoy spectacular sunset views over the lake and set up camp for the night.

Day 2 - Summit Ascent & Descent to the Lake

  • 02:00: Wake up call. Have a light breakfast (hot tea/coffee and biscuits) to prepare for the climb.
  • 02:30: Begin the challenging summit push through loose volcanic sand and gravel.
  • 06:00: Reach Mount Rinjani Summit (3,726m) for a breathtaking sunrise with views across to Bali and Sumbawa.
  • 07:00 – 09:00: Descend back to Sembalun Crater Rim for a hearty second breakfast.
  • 10:00 – 13:00: Begin the steep, rocky descent down into the caldera towards Segara Anak Lake (2,000m).
  • Afternoon: Arrive at the lake. Relax, have lunch, and visit the natural hot springs nearby to soak your tired muscles. Camp overnight by the lake.

Day 3 - Lake to Senaru Crater Rim

  • Morning: Enjoy a relaxed morning. You can sleep in slightly, explore the lakeside, or take another dip in the hot springs.
  • 12:00: Lunch is served at the campsite by the lake.
  • 13:00: Begin the climb out of the caldera towards the Senaru Crater Rim (2,641m). The trail is steep and rocky.
  • 16:00 – 17:00: Arrive at Senaru Crater Rim. Set up camp and enjoy incredible views of the new volcano (Mt. Baru Jari) and a spectacular sunset.

Day 4 - Descent to Senaru Village

  • 06:30: Wake up for sunrise over the crater rim. Enjoy a hearty breakfast.
  • 08:00: Begin the long descent towards Senaru Village. The first part is open and rocky, soon entering the dense, shady tropical rainforest.
  • 12:00: Stop for lunch at Pos 1 or a suitable resting area in the forest.
  • 13:30 – 14:30: Arrive at the Rinjani Trekking Centre in Senaru Village (601m), concluding your trek.
  • Afternoon: Our transport will be waiting to transfer you to your next destination in Lombok (Senggigi, Mataram, Bangsal Harbor, etc.).
