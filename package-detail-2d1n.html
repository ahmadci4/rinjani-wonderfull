Mount Rinjani 2 Days and 1 Night Summit Sembalun Route

Awaken the adventurer within. Your epic journey commences in the high-altitude agricultural heartland of Sembalun Village (1,100m). Trek across sweeping, sun-drenched savannas that offer vast, unobstructed panoramas before tackling the exhilarating ascent to the crater rim. To conquer the 2,639m rim, you will dig deep into your reserves to ascend the legendary "Regret Hills"—five steep inclines that test your spirit.

On the second day, the true test of endurance begins under a blanket of stars at 2:00 AM. Push your limits on the volcanic scree to stand atop the absolute pinnacle of Mount Rinjani (3,726m). Reaching the top of Indonesia's second-highest peak just as the first light of dawn illuminates the archipelago is a triumph you will carry forever. Though the descent follows the same path, managing your energy on the steep, loose gravel offers a thrilling new challenge.

Itinerary

Day 1 - Sembalun Village to Crater Rim

  • 06:00 – 06:30: Energizing breakfast at the hotel to fuel the big day.
  • 07:00 – 07:30: Transfer to Sembalun Village (approx. 45-minute scenic drive).
  • 08:30: Registration at the Rinjani National Park Office and officially commence the trek.
  • 12:00: Rest and enjoy a chef-prepared Lunch break at Post II.
  • 17:00: Triumphant arrival at Sembalun Crater Rim (Plawangan Sembalun, 2,639m). Witness a breathtaking sunset, enjoy a warm dinner, and camp overnight above the clouds.

Overview trail at day 1 - Sembalun village to crater rim:

Distance10 km
Duration6–7 hours
DifficultyModerate
Max Elevation2,639 m
Elevation Gain: +1,539 m

Day 2 - Summit Attack & Return to Sembalun

  • 02:00: Wake up under a starlit sky. Light breakfast with hot tea/coffee to warm your body.
  • 02:30: The ultimate push begins. Navigate the steep volcanic scree towards the peak.
  • 06:00: Reach the absolute Summit (3,726m) for a glorious sunrise over the Indonesian archipelago.
  • 07:00: Descend back to the Crater Rim campsite.
  • 09:30: Enjoy a hearty, well-deserved heavy breakfast.
  • 10:30 – 15:00: Trek back down through the savannas to Sembalun Village. Lunch served midway.
  • 15:00: Arrival at Sembalun. Our transport transfers you to your next destination.
