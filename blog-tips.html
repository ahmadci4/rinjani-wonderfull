Top 3 Golden Rules for Rinjani

Written by born-and-raised local guides. Follow these rules to guarantee your safety, comfort, and ultimate climbing triumph.

01

Master the Layering System

Rinjani temperatures swing wildly. You will sweat profusely under the 30°C tropical sun during the day, but temperatures drop near freezing (0°C to 5°C) at the summit and crater rims.

Pro Tip: Always pack a windproof jacket, thermal base layers, and a beanie. Let our porters carry your heavy gear while you keep only your light daypack!

02

Hydration & Electrolyte Strategy

Altitude sickness and exhaustion on Rinjani are often just severe dehydration in disguise. When climbing steep volcanic scree, your body loses fluids rapidly through heavy breathing.

Pro Tip: Drink at least 3 to 4 liters of water daily. Our in-house expedition packages include unlimited mineral water, fresh fruits, and hot ginger tea at every campsite!
