Rinjani Summit via Senaru (4 Days 3 Nights)

4 Days 3 Nights

Ascend through lush rainforests before reaching the majestic summit. This reverse route (Senaru to Sembalun) is beloved by trekkers who prefer to tackle the mountain under the cool, shaded canopy of the tropical jungle on their first day, rather than the exposed savannas of Sembalun.

This 4-day, 3-night expedition is perfectly paced. You will be rewarded early on with a spectacular sunset over the crater rim, followed by a full day of relaxation at the stunning Segara Anak Lake and therapeutic hot springs. Fully acclimatized and rested, you will traverse to the Sembalun rim to mount your final, dramatic push to the 3,726m summit on your last morning, descending finally through the rolling plains of Sembalun.

About this activity

  • The "Jungle First" Route

    Begin in the shade of dense tropical forests, shielding you from the midday sun, making the initial climb cooler and highly scenic.

  • Maximum Acclimatization

    By leaving the summit for the final day, your body has 3 days to adjust to altitude and recover at the hot springs.

  • All-Inclusive Convenience

    Expert guides, porters for equipment/food, pre-trek hotel, and all meals are meticulously arranged for you.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1 - Senaru Village to Senaru Crater Rim

  • 06:30 – 07:30: Breakfast at your hotel in Senaru.
  • 07:30: Register at the Rinjani Trekking Centre (RTC) in Senaru (601m).
  • 08:00: Begin the trek ascending through dense tropical forest rich in flora, bird life, and butterflies. Look out for the rare black ebony leaf monkey (Lutung).
  • 12:00: Lunch break at Pos 2 (Montong Satas, 1,500m).
  • 16:30 – 17:00: Reach the spectacular Senaru Crater Rim (2,641m). Enjoy sweeping sunset views over Segara Anak Lake, Mount Agung in Bali, and the Gili Islands. Camp overnight.

Day 2 - Senaru Rim to Segara Anak Lake

  • 07:00: Wake up to a stunning sunrise, enjoy a hearty breakfast prepared by your team.
  • 08:00 – 10:00: Begin the descent down the steep and rocky trail to Segara Anak Lake (2,000m).
  • 10:30 – Afternoon: Arrive at the lake edge. The rest of the day is yours to relax. Visit the natural hot springs just a 10-minute walk away—perfect for soothing your muscles.
  • Evening: Dinner and camping right beside the serene crater lake, shadowed by the active volcano Mt. Baru Jari.

Day 3 - Lake to Sembalun Crater Rim

  • Morning: Take a leisurely breakfast. You have extra time this morning for one final soak in the hot springs or simply enjoying the lakeside view.
  • 12:00: Lunch is served at the lake campsite.
  • 13:30 – 16:30: Begin the steep, zig-zagging climb out of the caldera towards the Sembalun Crater Rim (2,639m).
  • 17:00: Arrive at Sembalun Crater Rim (Basecamp). From here, gaze upon the summit you will tackle tomorrow. Have an early dinner and sleep early to prepare for the night ascent.

Day 4 - Summit Push & Descent to Sembalun

  • 02:00: Wake up! Grab hot drinks and a light snack for energy.
  • 02:30: Begin the strenuous climb to the summit over loose volcanic scree. It requires physical and mental stamina.
  • 06:00: Reach the Mount Rinjani Summit (3,726m) for a breathtaking sunrise with panoramic views of Indonesia.
  • 07:00 – 09:00: Descend back to the Sembalun Rim camp for a massive, well-earned breakfast.
  • 10:00 – 14:30: Long descent through the open, sun-drenched savannas to Sembalun Village. Lunch is provided on the trail.
  • Afternoon: Arrive at Sembalun Village. Our vehicle will be waiting to transfer you to your next destination in Lombok.
