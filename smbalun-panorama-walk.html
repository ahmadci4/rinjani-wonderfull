EXPLORE SEMBALUN

Half / Full Day

Step away from the strenuous alpine ascents and immerse yourself in the tranquil beauty of the Sembalun Valley. Situated at an elevation of 1,100 meters, this picturesque village is cradled by the majestic foothills of Mount Rinjani, offering a serene escape filled with agricultural wonders and local heritage.

The Explore Sembalun is a beautifully curated, relaxed-pace journey designed for all ages. Wander through patchwork quilt fields, breathe in the crisp mountain air, and engage directly with the vibrant local farming community. From plucking fresh strawberries to exploring enchanting bamboo groves, this tour is a deep dive into the peaceful rhythm of Sasak mountain life.

Duration

3 - 6 Hours

(Half day or Full day options)

Difficulty

Easy / Relaxed

Starting Point

Sembalun Village

Tour Highlights

  • Scenic Hill Viewpoints

    Enjoy unobstructed, panoramic views of the entire Sembalun caldera and the imposing Mount Rinjani backdrop.

  • Local Strawberry Farms

    Visit local plantations where you can pick and taste sweet, mountain-grown strawberries straight from the vine.

  • Traditional Sasak Heritage

    Discover traditional weaving techniques and learn about the unique agricultural lifestyle of the Sembalun people.
